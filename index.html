The Terrace

Marrakech, from its
most beautiful angle

Perched above the bustle of the medina, Nour Rooftop serves the city the way it deserves to be seen — from above. By day, a sunlit terrace for long lunches. By night, lanterns, live music and the lights of the medina below.

The kitchen stays close to Moroccan classics — tagines that fall apart, generous couscous, pastilla with cinnamon — with Mediterranean touches for every table.
